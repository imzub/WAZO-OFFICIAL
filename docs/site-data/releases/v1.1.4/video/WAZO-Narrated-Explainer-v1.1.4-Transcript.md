# WAZO Narrated Explainer v1.1.4 — English Transcript

Wealth can be spread across accounts, properties, investments, goals, and family members. WAZO brings the complete picture together in one Windows app.

WAZO is built for individuals and families who want a clear, private way to organize their financial life, without depending on an always-online service.

Start with family profiles and assign each asset to its real owner. The portfolio then shows net worth, holdings, and allocation across the household, while keeping ownership easy to understand.

Use the Asset Library to define reusable asset types, then add investments and other holdings consistently. This keeps your records organized as your portfolio grows.

For SIP plans and recurring investments, WAZO prepares upcoming entries for review. Check the details first, then confirm them when they are correct, so your holdings change intentionally.

Set financial goals and allocation targets, then follow progress over time. Performance views help you understand gains and losses, financial-year results, CAGR, and XIRR.

When you need to share or analyze information, create clear reports and export them to PDF, Excel, CSV, JSON, or print.

Your core records stay on your computer in local app storage. Privacy Mode helps conceal sensitive values, while manual and scheduled backups, plus restore previews, help protect your data.

Online rate updates are optional and used only when you enable them. Optional zakat tools can also be turned on when they are relevant to you.

From everyday organization to long-term planning, WAZO gives you one calm, structured view of your wealth. Discover WAZO for Windows and take control of your financial picture.

## Version note

The interface was recorded in WAZO v1.1.4 with anonymous demo data. The current v1.1.5 Windows release retains these core workflows and adds guided privacy setup and protected Windows storage.
