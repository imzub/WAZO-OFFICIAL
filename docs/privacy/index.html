WAZO Privacy Policy

WAZO is a local-first personal wealth organizer developed by Zubair Shaikh. It processes the information you enter on your Windows device so it can organize your finances. Using the app does not, by itself, send your portfolio to the developer.

01

Scope and responsibility

This policy describes privacy handling in WAZO 1.1.5 for Windows and the WAZO Official website. Zubair Shaikh is the developer responsible for WAZO. WAZO is designed for personal or family wealth organization and is not a bank, broker, investment adviser, payment service, tax service, or cloud account provider.

If you record information about another family member, you are responsible for having an appropriate reason and permission to do so. The device owner controls the Windows account, local files, selected backup locations, and exported reports.

02

Information WAZO processes

Depending on the features you use, WAZO may process these categories locally:

Profiles and members

Family or profile names, member names, ownership assignments, and related profile preferences.

Assets and valuations

Asset definitions, categories, ownership, quantities, values, prices, rates, allocation targets, notes, and optional reference labels.

Plans and transactions

Investment plans, schedules, pending occurrences, confirmed transactions, cost basis, performance calculations, CAGR, and XIRR inputs and results.

Goals and optional Zakat

Financial goals, target values and dates, and—only if enabled—Zakat settings, calculations, eligibility details, and payments.

Preferences and diagnostics

Currency, country, regional format, financial year, theme, privacy settings, backup settings, and local activity or diagnostic event logs.

Files and recovery data

Imports, app-managed backups, transaction generations, migration or recovery copies, damaged-data evidence, reports, and user-initiated exports or print output.

Policy records

The accepted policy revision, effective date, document hash, app version, acceptance source, and a timestamp created by the app.

Support content

Only the information you voluntarily include if you contact the developer, such as your email address, question, diagnostic details, or attachments.

03

Why WAZO processes it

WAZO uses locally stored information to:

  • create family profiles, assign member ownership, and display your chosen regional and currency formats;
  • calculate portfolio values, allocation, goals, recurring occurrences, returns, and optional Zakat;
  • create the reports, prints, and exports that you request;
  • remember your settings and current privacy-policy acknowledgement;
  • protect data integrity through transaction generations, recovery points, validation, and backups; and
  • show local activity and diagnostic information that helps you understand or troubleshoot the app.

WAZO does not sell or rent personal information and does not use portfolio data for advertising.

04

Storage and protection on Windows

WAZO stores app-managed active data inside the current user’s Windows application-data area. In WAZO 1.1.5, active records, app-managed transaction generations, recovery data, logs, and internal backups containing personal or financial data are protected with authenticated encryption. The local protection key is secured through Electron’s Windows safe-storage integration, which uses Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI).

What Windows-bound protection means

It is designed to prevent readable app data from being left as ordinary local files and to detect unauthorized changes. The protected key is normally usable only by the same Windows user on the same Windows installation. It is not a substitute for a strong Windows password, full-device encryption, malware protection, or physical device security. Software already running with your Windows user’s privileges may still be able to access information while WAZO is open. Losing the Windows account or device can make the local protected store unrecoverable without a portable backup.

Privacy Mode is different

Privacy Mode redacts sensitive values from supported screens, previews, accessibility content, print, and exports. It is useful when sharing a screen or creating a redacted output, but it is not encryption, authentication, access control, secure deletion, or a replacement for locking your Windows session.

No security measure can guarantee absolute protection. Keep Windows updated, protect your account, use trusted software, and store portable-backup passwords safely.

05

Policy acknowledgement

During initial setup, WAZO shows a privacy summary and link to this policy before it stores real personal or financial data. The acknowledgement control is not selected in advance. A new user may continue later in demo-only mode; attempts to add real data reopen setup.

When an existing installation needs a material policy or storage update, WAZO asks the user to review and acknowledge the current policy before transforming or exposing existing local portfolio data. If the user exits or declines, WAZO leaves the prior store unchanged.

WAZO records the policy version, effective date, policy-document hash, app version, source, and app-generated UTC timestamp locally. This record shows that the local notice was accepted; it is not proof of identity and does not authorize the developer to collect a portfolio.

06

Backups, recovery, exports, and print

App-managed recovery data

WAZO may create protected transaction generations, pre-migration or pre-restore recovery points, and internal backups to prevent partial or failed changes from destroying active data. These remain under WAZO’s local-data controls.

Portable backups

Windows-bound local protection cannot move safely to a different Windows account or device. WAZO therefore offers a separate password-protected portable backup format. You choose the password and destination. WAZO and the developer cannot recover a forgotten backup password. Anyone with the file and password may be able to restore its contents, so protect both.

User-selected exports and destinations

PDF, Excel, CSV, JSON, HTML, print output, screenshots, and other files you deliberately create may be readable and are outside WAZO’s protected application storage. Files saved to OneDrive, another cloud-synced folder, removable media, email, or a shared location may be processed by those services or seen by other people. WAZO displays an export warning, but you remain responsible for selecting a safe destination and deleting copies you no longer need.

07

Optional internet features and external providers

Core portfolio organization works locally. Online rate updates are off by default and require the user to enable them. When enabled, the selected rate endpoint receives ordinary internet connection information—such as an IP address, request time, and transport metadata—plus the requested symbol or rate parameters. WAZO does not include family names, holdings, transaction history, portfolio values, goals, Zakat records, or local portfolio files in a rate request.

Do not place a password, token, private key, or other secret in a custom rate URL. Turning online rates off stops WAZO from making rate-provider requests.

Opening the WAZO Official website, Microsoft Store, LinkedIn, or a support-email link transfers control to your browser, Microsoft, LinkedIn, GitHub Pages, or your email provider. Those services may receive normal connection, device, account, and message information under their own privacy terms. The WAZO Official website does not receive your local portfolio merely because you visit it.

08

Access, retention, reset, and deletion

Access and correction

Use WAZO’s profile, Assets, Asset Library, goals, reports, optional Zakat, and Settings screens to review or edit supported records. Export tools can create a copy of selected information in a format you choose.

How long information remains

Active data, preferences, policy metadata, logs, and app-managed recovery information remain on the device while needed for the app’s features, integrity, and recovery, until replaced through normal app operation or removed through reset or permanent deletion. User-selected backups and exports remain wherever you saved them until you delete them. Support communications remain in the relevant email systems as needed to answer or manage the request.

Reset to demo

Reset to demo is intended to replace active records with sample data and reopen initial setup. It may first create a protected recovery point. Reset is not the same as permanent deletion.

Permanent local deletion

WAZO’s permanent-delete control removes the active protected store, app-managed generations and recovery data, local policy acknowledgement, and app-managed automatic backups after explicit confirmation. It cannot find or delete user-selected backups, exports, printed copies, email attachments, screenshots, Windows File History, cloud-provider copies, or third-party backups. Storage hardware and operating-system behavior may also prevent a guarantee of forensic erasure.

Uninstalling WAZO

Windows or the installer may preserve per-user application data after an uninstall. If you intend to remove your records, use WAZO’s permanent-delete control before uninstalling, then separately remove any backups or exports you created elsewhere.

09

Information you should not enter

WAZO does not need and does not ask you to record:

  • bank or payment-card login credentials or full payment-card details;
  • PINs, passwords, one-time codes, or authentication answers;
  • tax identification numbers;
  • API keys, private keys, or access tokens; or
  • cryptocurrency wallet recovery phrases, seed words, or private keys.

Optional account or reference fields are descriptive labels only. Do not use them to store secrets or credentials.

10

Support communications

If you email support, the developer receives the sender address and whatever text or attachments you choose to include. That information is used to respond, investigate the reported issue, and maintain necessary support records. Email providers process the message under their own terms.

Remove or redact portfolio values and other sensitive information before sending unless it is necessary for the request and you intentionally choose to include it. WAZO never sends a local portfolio automatically as part of support.

11

Changes to this policy

This page shows its revision and effective date. Material changes that affect how WAZO processes, protects, shares, or retains information require a new policy revision. The app may ask users to review and acknowledge that revision before real data is newly stored or migrated under changed terms. Minor editorial corrections may be published without changing app behavior.
